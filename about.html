Simple and practical utilities for everyday tasks

SPBasic Tools is a collection of small utilities built to do one job quickly and without fuss. Merge or compress a PDF, optimize an image, record audio or video, or generate a QR code. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device. There are no installs and no sign-ups. Open a tool and get the job done.

What you can do here

PDF
Merge, split, watermark, reorder and compress PDF files.
Image
Convert and shrink high-resolution pictures.
Audio
Record from your microphone or mark timestamps to compile custom cuts.
Video
Record camera video with audio and download it.
Utility
Generate QR codes from text, URLs and other data.

Why they're private

Our tools process your files with code that runs in your own browser. Your documents, images and recordings are not uploaded to a server.

We add new utilities over time, always with the same goal: tools that are fast, free and easy to use. Missing something you need? Tell us.
