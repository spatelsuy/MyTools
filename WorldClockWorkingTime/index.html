How to use World Clock & Time Zone Planner

  1. Add time zones. Your own zone is first (by default). Click + to add more, or × on a tile to remove one.
  2. Set working hours. Change Works __ to __ (24-hour, e.g. 9 to 17) on each tile. The green bars show when each zone is working.
  3. Meeting time. Click or drag on a chart to move the meeting box. Each zone shows its own time and day.
  4. Set the meeting length. Drag the grip on the right edge of the box, or use the minutes box with the up/down arrows (easier on a phone). The box starts at 30 min.
  5. Change the day. Use ‹ ›, the date box, or scroll sideways. Live returns to now.
  6. Schedule it. Schedule in Outlook opens a new event with your local time zone. Download .ics works with any calendar.

Your zones and hours are saved in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded. Click ? any time to see this again.
